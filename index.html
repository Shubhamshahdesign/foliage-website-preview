Proudly independent

A small café in the middle of Clifton Village.

Foliage opened on Regent Street in 2019. Inside you'll find hanging plants, warm lights, a long window bar for watching the street, and a counter stacked with pastries, cakes and sandwiches.

The kitchen keeps things simple: good bread, fresh toppings and plates that work for a quick breakfast or a slow weekend brunch. Coffee comes in our blue and yellow cups, with alternative milks and Chemex on the board.

Sit in, take away, or grab one of the yellow stools outside when the sun's out.
